# Content Validation Workflow

## APA Citation Verification

### Required Format
- All citations must follow APA 7th edition format
- Minimum 40% of sources must be peer-reviewed
- All external links must be verified and active

### Verification Checklist
- [ ] All technical claims have verifiable sources
- [ ] Citations follow APA format
- [ ] Minimum 40% peer-reviewed sources requirement met
- [ ] All external links are active
- [ ] Content follows Docusaurus-compatible Markdown format

### Common APA Citation Formats

**Journal Article:**
Author, A. A. (Year). Title of article. *Title of Periodical*, volume(issue), pages. https://doi.org/xx.xxx/yyyy

**Book:**
Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of work*. Publisher.

**Website:**
Author, A. A. (Year, Month Date). Title of webpage. *Website Name*. URL

**Conference Paper:**
Author, A. A. (Year). Title of paper. *Title of Conference*, pages. Publisher. URL
